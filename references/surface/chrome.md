# メッキ（CHROME）

- プレースホルダー：なし
- 照明：REFLECTIVE

## 基本ブロック

```text
The surface is true mirror chrome. Chrome has no color of its own — its appearance is entirely the reflection of the studio environment. Build the form through reflection banding, not through painted shading.

- Render classic light-dark-light banding: a clean elongated white softbox streak along the upper curvature, a distinct darker gradient band below it (reflection of the black negative-fill card), then a lighter bounce band near the lower edge. This banding IS the three-dimensionality.
- Specular highlights must retain a gradient. Do NOT clip to flat pure white — keep highlights below RGB 250 except for tiny pinpoint catchlights.
- Reflected environment must be abstract soft studio gradients only. No recognizable room, furniture, photographer, camera, or window shapes.
- Add a thin darker edge definition line along the silhouette so the chrome does not dissolve into the background.
- Reflect a subtle graduated gray tone along the lower body for background separation.
- Keep the plating flawless: no pitting, no orange peel, no haze, no fingerprints.
```

## バリエーション

### BLACK CHROME（ブラックメッキ・スモークメッキ）— 置き換え：冒頭の段落

> 状態：草案（実際の生成で未確認）

```text
The surface is black (smoked) chrome: a true mirror finish with a dark gunmetal tint. Its appearance is the reflection of the studio environment, darkened by the tint. Build the form through reflection banding, not through painted shading.
- Highlights read gray-white rather than pure white; the dark bands go deep charcoal.
- Still a mirror — not matte, not gray paint.
```
